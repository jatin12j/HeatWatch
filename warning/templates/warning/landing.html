BIOMETEOROLOGICAL FOUNDATION

Why Standard Temperature Readings Lie

A reading of 38°C in dry desert heat is physically tolerable, while 38°C at 75% relative humidity in coastal India is fatal within hours. HeatWatch calculates physiological stress based on thermodynamics.

🌡️

Dry-Bulb Air Temp

The raw temperature measured by a standard shielded thermometer. It completely ignores atmospheric moisture, radiative heat, and evaporative cooling.

Limitation: Inadequate for health warnings during tropical or pre-monsoon heat events.
🔥

Rothfusz Heat Index

9-variable multivariate polynomial modeling human skin evaporation. It computes the "feels-like" temperature that triggers heat cramps and heat exhaustion.

Formula: NOAA Rothfusz regression with low/high RH adjustments.
⚠️

Stull Wet-Bulb (Tw)

The absolute thermodynamic limit of human survivability. At a wet-bulb temperature of 35°C, human sweat cannot evaporate, causing core body temp to spike lethally.

Formula: Stull (2011) psychrometric arctangent model.
TECHNICAL CAPABILITIES

Engineered for Rapid Disaster Response

Explore the complete suite of predictive and preventative tools integrated into HeatWatch.

🌐
Zero-Key Live Weather Ingestion

Pulls hourly meteorological datasets (temperature, relative humidity, wind speed) directly from the open Open-Meteo API for 12 vulnerable Indian hubs.

👥
Demographic Vulnerability Offsets

Applies a calibrated -3°C threshold delta for elderly citizens, young children, and outdoor workers whose thermoregulatory systems degrade faster.

📅
3-Day Consecutive Run Detection

Detects sustained heat episodes (3 consecutive days at Danger or above) in accordance with the Indian Meteorological Department (IMD) heatwave definitions.

🗺️
Interactive Geospatial Risk Map

Leaflet-powered dark canvas map with pulsing danger markers, layer controls (Satellite, Dark, Standard), and verified municipal cooling shelter routing.

📊
168-Hour Granular Time-Series

High-density Chart.js visual graphs plotting hourly dry-bulb temp, calculated heat index, and wet-bulb curves against physiological danger thresholds.

🧮
Instant Thermal Stress Calculator

Allows field workers, first responders, and citizens to enter any custom temperature and humidity to receive real-time risk classification and medical advice.

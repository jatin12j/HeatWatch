Heat Safety Guidelines

Heat-related illness is preventable. Know the risk levels, warning signs, and life-saving actions.

Below 27°C HI
✅ Normal
  • Conditions are comfortable
  • Stay hydrated as general practice
  • Enjoy outdoor activities normally
27–32°C HI
⚠️ Caution
  • Fatigue possible with prolonged exposure
  • Drink water regularly
  • Take shade breaks outdoors
32–39°C HI
🟠 Extreme Caution
  • Heat cramps and exhaustion possible
  • Avoid strenuous work 12–4 PM
  • Wear light, loose-fitting clothing
  • Drink water every 15–20 minutes
39–51°C HI
🔴 Danger
  • Heat stroke is likely
  • No outdoor activities 12–4 PM
  • Stay in AC/cooled spaces
  • Check on elderly & children
  • Never leave anyone in parked cars
Above 51°C HI
⛔ Extreme Danger
  • HEAT STROKE IMMINENT
  • STAY INDOORS at all costs
  • If outdoors — minutes only
  • Drink water constantly
  • Go to nearest cooling center
Signs of Heat Exhaustion
  • Heavy sweating, cold/pale/clammy skin
  • Fast, weak pulse
  • Nausea, vomiting, muscle cramps
  • Tiredness, dizziness, headache, fainting
Move to cool place, loosen clothes, sip water, apply cool cloths. Seek help if symptoms last over 1 hour.
Signs of Heat Stroke (EMERGENCY)
  • Body temp above 40°C (104°F)
  • Hot, red, dry or damp skin — NO sweating
  • Fast, strong pulse
  • Confusion, headache, loss of consciousness
CALL 112 IMMEDIATELY!
Apply cool cloths, cool bath. Do NOT give fluids if unconscious.
